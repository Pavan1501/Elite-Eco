About Us

Our Story

Founded with a vision to combat plastic pollution, Elite Eco Recycling has grown from a small initiative into a leading PET recycling company. Our journey began with a mission to create environmental change by converting waste into valuable, reusable resources.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to sustainability and innovation. Our mission is to lead the way in plastic recycling by delivering high-quality PET flakes and promoting eco-conscious industrial practices for a cleaner, greener future.

Our Products

Clear PET Flakes

High-quality recycled PET flakes from post-consumer bottles,ideal for food-grade applications and textile production.

HDPE Flakes

Durable and versatile HDPE flakes recycled from containers and bottles, suitable for pipe manufacturing and industrial containers.

PP Flakes

Recycled polypropylene flakes from various sources, perfect for furniture, automotive parts, and consumer goods.
